# ITAI_4375
Individual portfilio for HCC class ITAI 4375 AI in Healthcare

# Healthcare AI and Data Assignments

## A01 – Medical Terminology Identification
Review recent medical articles or healthcare reports and identify at least 10 medical terms. For each term, define it, explain the context it was used in, and describe why it’s important in healthcare.

## A02 – Mock Healthcare Dataset
Work with a mock healthcare dataset to explore patient information. Calculate basic statistics like average age and heart rate, showing how you got your results.

## A03 – Future AI-Powered Clinical Decision Support
Imagine a future (5–10 years from now) where AI-powered Clinical Decision Support Systems like Glass Health are widely used. Describe how these systems work, how clinicians use them, and the benefits, challenges, and ethical considerations.

## A04 – Understanding a Medical Test
Research a medical test and explain it in simple terms. Describe what it tests for, how accurate it is, potential errors, and who might need it. Share your opinion on the test’s usefulness.

## A05 – Critiquing a Prognostic Model
Review a study that uses a prognostic model. Explain what it predicts, the factors it uses, its accuracy, strengths, and weaknesses. Share your opinion on how useful the model is.

## A06 – Non-Traditional Health Data
Research either genetic testing (like 23andMe) or social determinants of health (SDOH). Explain what the data is, how it can improve health outcomes, and any ethical or privacy concerns.

## A07 – Identifying Risks in a Case Study
Analyze a medical case study and identify at least three potential health risks. Explain why each is important, what could happen if not addressed, and how AI could help manage these risks.

## A08 – Critiquing NLP in Healthcare
Review a healthcare NLP application that extracts data from electronic health records (EHRs). Explain NLP, describe two benefits and two limitations, and share your opinion on its usefulness.

## A09 – Designing Reinforcement Learning Scenarios
Design a simple Reinforcement Learning (RL) scenario to improve patient care. Explain RL, describe your scenario, define state, action, and reward, discuss benefits and challenges, and reflect on RL’s impact.

## Midterm – AI in Healthcare Glossary
Create a glossary of at least 15 AI terms in healthcare. For each term, provide a clear definition, an example, and its significance. Include an introduction, conclusion, and organize the glossary alphabetically.
